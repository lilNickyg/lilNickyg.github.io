Flight heritage / January 2026

Wrapped‑Rib: designed on Earth, proven in orbit.

At Oxford Space Systems I was Mechanical Design Lead and a work-package technical lead within the team developing the Wrapped‑Rib deployable antenna.

Integrated with SSTL’s CarbSAR spacecraft, it launched on a SpaceX Falcon 9 on 11 January 2026, deployed successfully in orbit and is now operating—turning years of design, analysis, manufacture and ground testing into flight heritage.
